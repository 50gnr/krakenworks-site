Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026

Piper Mail is a private, owner-operated email assistant used only with Google accounts that the account owner explicitly authorizes. It is not offered as a public service.

Information accessed

After Google OAuth authorization, Piper Mail may access Gmail messages, threads, sender and recipient information, labels, timestamps, and attachments. It accesses only the Google services and accounts approved during authorization. Google account passwords are never collected or stored.

How information is used

Authorized Gmail data is used only to perform owner-requested mailbox tasks, such as searching and summarizing messages, identifying items that need attention, organizing mail, preparing drafts, and downloading requested attachments. Actions such as sending, deleting, archiving, labeling, or unsubscribing are performed only under rules or approvals established by the account owner.

AI processing and service providers

Email content may be processed by the self-hosted OpenClaw assistant and its configured AI model providers solely to provide requested features. Current providers may include OpenAI and Anthropic. These providers process data under their applicable service terms and privacy commitments. Gmail data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared for unrelated purposes.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in the owner's private, self-hosted environment using protected credential storage. Gmail remains stored by Google unless the owner directs a mailbox action. Downloaded attachments and generated summaries may be retained locally for the owner's projects or records and removed according to the owner's retention choices. Authorization records are retained until access is revoked or the integration is removed.

Security

Piper Mail uses Google OAuth rather than account passwords. Access is limited to approved accounts, requested scopes, and the owner's secured OpenClaw environment. Reasonable technical safeguards are used to restrict network, device, and credential access.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Piper Mail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your controls

The account owner can review or revoke access at any time through Google Account third-party connections. The owner may also request deletion of locally stored OAuth credentials, downloaded attachments, or generated mailbox data.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Piper Mail's features, providers, or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

Contact

Questions about Piper Mail or this policy can be sent to admin@krakenworks.app.
